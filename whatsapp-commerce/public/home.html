Questions

Do I need the WhatsApp Business API?

For the full chat shop and AI, yes – we connect it for you through Meta's official sign-up in a few minutes. You can also start in "lite" mode: a storefront where orders arrive in your normal WhatsApp.

Does it work with my Shopify or WooCommerce shop?

Yes. Products, prices and stock are read live, and orders are created in your shop – so fulfilment, stock and accounting work exactly as today.

What does WhatsApp itself charge?

Meta charges per marketing/utility template message (pennies in the UK). Replies to customers who message you are free. These fees are billed by Meta directly.

Can the AI make mistakes?

It only uses your real catalogue and the policies you give it, and hands over to your team when it's unsure or the customer asks for a person. You can review every chat in the inbox.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes – manage or cancel your subscription from the Billing page. No contracts.

Is my data safe?

Credentials are encrypted, webhooks are verified, and we act as your data processor under a UK GDPR data processing agreement.
